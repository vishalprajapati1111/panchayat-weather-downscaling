THE GAP, AND WHAT PARAM DOES ABOUT IT
Across 80 km of the Western Ghats, rain falls from 3,300 mm at the coast to 318.1 mm inland — a tenfold collapse, and the grid cannot see it.
CURRENT PROBLEMS
Forecasts arrive in 28 km squares.
One number covers a coastal strip, an escarpment and a rain shadow — all with wildly different rainfall.
Advisories are issued at block level.
The panchayat that needs the number is two orders of magnitude smaller than the grid that produces it.
The Ghats break interpolation.
Farmers receive weather, not a decision.
"ETo 5.2 mm/day" is not an answer to "do I irrigate today". Numbers without context do not reach the field.
TERRAIN CROSS-SECTION — 250° MONSOON INFLOW · SRTM 30 m BASE
Ten times less rain. Ninety minutes' drive. One forecast number.
A FROZEN TERRAIN LENS — Hills never move, so we solve the terrain once at 250 m detail from SRTM 30 m. That single solve resolves three times more rainfall spread (2.14 → 6.44 mm) hiding inside one 28 km cell.
MASS-CONSERVING DISAGGREGATION — Inside every 0.25° cell the village ratios rescale so their area-weighted mean is exactly 1.0. The worst cell in the whole domain is off by 4.44 × 10−16.
TWO CLOCKS, FLAT COST — Terrain work runs once, ever. The nightly run covers all 16,943 villages in 8.8 s, and ten users or ten lakh users cost the same.
A DECISION, NOT A DATA POINT — Every village gets its own rainfall, Tmax, Tmin and crop water demand, plus a flag saying whether we validated there. Tmax error fell from 2.12 °C to 1.53 °C on IFS forecasts.
ECMWF IFS 0.25° is the live driver for every forecast in this deck. The IMD API is the designated national primary in our ingestion hierarchy. ERA5 is used for calibration and as the degraded-mode floor, never as the live input.

FEASIBILITY, RISKS AND WHAT WE DO NOT CLAIM
We never invent rain: each cell's total is preserved to double-precision 4.44 × 10−16.
FEASIBILITY
RUNS ON COMMODITY HARDWARE
Full domain, 16,943 villages in 8.8 s end to end on a laptop. No GPU, no cluster, no supercomputer time.
COST IS FLAT IN USERS
The nightly job runs once for the whole state, and the app reads one ready row. Serving cost does not grow as users grow.
DRIVER-AGNOSTIC INGESTION
The lens does not care where the forecast comes from. Swapping IMD for ECMWF, or either for a future AI weather model, changes two config lines and no science.
ALWAYS AN ANSWER
Three source tiers with automatic failover. If a feed is late the farmer sees a labelled climate value, not an empty screen.
CHALLENGES & MITIGATION
CHALLENGEMITIGATION
Sparse gauge network19 GHCN gauges; validated LOSO; villages carry in-band/extrapolated flags.
Out-of-season flow reversalContinuous upslope gate damps lens outside 250° sector.
Forecast feed outageFailover to ECMWF IFS, then ERA5; source named on screen.
Unverifiable claimsRainfall, temperature and ETo are reproducible from the committed precomputed CSV and git commit 054561a. The wind-gate generation script is not committed; only the precomputed gate_g column is.
Serving cost rising with user numbers.The nightly job runs once for the whole domain in 8.8 s and the app reads one prepared row, so cost does not grow as users grow.
Getting departments to adopt a new tool.Our output is a table and an API, not another app. Existing advisory channels consume it without changing how they reach farmers.
We got worse in one place. Here it is.
−14.4 %
Tmin correction was 14.4 % worse on 1,500 in-band station-days; disabled. Tmin is served from physics alone.
Rainfall median APE: 9.53 % windward / 32.03 % lee / 16.09 % pooled (spread disclosed: Hulikal 50.6 %, Agumbe Obsy 27.7 %).
Villages outside 12.8–15.3 °N are flagged 'Extrapolated'. The flag is part of the product.
The learned correction only sees weather and time-of-day inputs, nothing village-specific. So it fixes forecast bias at cell scale and adds no village-scale detail of its own.
6.5 °C/km lapse rate is steeper than the monsoon profile (≈5 °C/km); high villages are over-cooled.
We do not model night-time cold-air pooling in valleys, so our day-to-night temperature swing is narrower than the real one.
